Key Results from NSphere

Explore these results online in our Example 1 Notebook which shows how to create phase-space and density profile animations.

Initial Phase Space Distribution: The initial phase space distribution 16π²r²v²f(r,v) versus radius r and velocity v, generated by sampling the theoretical DF using 200,000,000 particles. Particle Trajectories: Example of the "orbits" in the simulation. More precisely, trajectories described by radius as a function of time, for 1000 randomly chosen particles in a simulation of 100,000 particles. Decaying Dark Matter Evolution: Time evolution of the mass distribution (∝ r² ρ(r) in dimensionless units) of an initially self-consistent self-gravitating system with decaying dark matter. In each time step of width Δt, each particle has a chance Γ Δt to receive a randomly oriented velocity change of magnitude Δv=100 km/s. The lifetime Γ⁻¹ has been taken to be twice the integration time of 20 dynamical times, with 1000 time steps per dynamical time. 10⁶ particles in this simulation.
Variable Newton's Constant G: Time evolution of the mass distribution (∝ r² ρ(r) in dimensionless units) of an initially self-consistent self-gravitating system with a time evolving Newton's constant G. Here, G decreases linearly by a factor of 2 over the integration time. Tidal Stripping Evolution: Time evolution of the mass distribution (∝ r² ρ(r) in dimensionless units) of a self-consistent self-gravitating system that has had the outer 40% of its mass stripped. The system was evolved with 600,000 particles for 20 dynamical times with 1000 time steps per dynamical time. Curves are smoothed with a Gaussian filter to reduce shot-noise, but the initial conditions are a sharp cutoff. The system relaxes to a density profile that is well approximated by ρ(r) ∝ (r²+b²)⁻⁵. SMBH Growth: Evolution of the density distribution (in dimensionless units) of a self-consistent self-gravitating system in which a supermassive black hole is slowly grown to 0.1 times the total halo mass over the integration time of 20 dynamical times.
Phase-Space After Tidal Stripping: The phase-space distribution of the halo right after tidal stripping of the outer 40% of particles. Phase-Space After 25 Dynamical Times: The phase-space distribution after 25 dynamical times showing the relaxed state of the tidally stripped system.

Please see arXiv:2504.13996 for further details.
